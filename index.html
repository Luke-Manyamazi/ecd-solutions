Common questions

Before you get in touch

Quick answers for centre owners and directors.

Can I use only one of the seven services?+

Yes. We can support one priority area or help you work through the full development cycle depending on your needs and budget.

Do you guarantee ECD registration or certification?+

No. We provide readiness guidance, checklists and preparation support. Registration and certification remain the responsibility of the relevant authorities.

Do you provide first-aid and fire certification directly?+

Specialist first-aid and fire training should be delivered by appropriately qualified providers. ECD Centre Solutions can coordinate suitable providers and integrate that support into the centre's wider safety plan.

Can you help centres outside Masvingo?+

Contact us with your location in Zimbabwe and the support you need. We can discuss what can be delivered directly, remotely or through coordinated service providers.
